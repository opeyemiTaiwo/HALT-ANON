# Extended results

Tables referenced in the paper as held in the released artifact. Every
number is regenerated by `pipeline/run_all.sh`; the source CSVs are in
this directory.


## A. Hallucination taxonomy

| Type | Axis | Description |
|---|---|---|
| H1 Scene Fabrication | Fabrication | Describes a setting or event with no source in the footage |
| H5 Entity Fabrication | Fabrication | Introduces objects or details not present |
| H6 Phantom Actors | Fabrication | Introduces people who do not appear |
| H3 Crime Missed | Omission | Fails to report the criminal act under investigation |
| H2 Crime Misclassification | Distortion | Reports the act as a different category of crime |
| H4 Severity Minimization | Distortion | Understates the gravity of the act |

the download. The pipeline uses the full file.
